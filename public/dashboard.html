⚑ Options carry meaningfully more risk than the ideas above — leverage, time decay, assignment — and your broker requires separate approval to trade them. These are strategy-level research prompts only, never specific strikes or premiums (no live options-chain data feeds this). Look up real pricing yourself before doing anything. Requires the Pro plan ($40/mo).

Day Trading Setups Pro

⚑ This is the highest-risk category of idea this app produces. Most retail day traders lose money over time — this isn't a disclaimer to skip past, it's the actual base rate. There's no live intraday data feeding this: "watch for" levels are real, searched technical context, never live ticks, and never a directive to buy or sell at a specific time. Verify everything against real-time charts yourself before acting on anything here. Requires the Pro plan ($40/mo).
